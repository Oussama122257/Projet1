# ContentLoop — Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-27_

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs use of the ContentLoop platform (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to this AUP and to our Terms of Service.

## 1. Content rights — the core rule

You may only import, store, process, schedule, or publish content that **you own** or for which you hold **explicit permission or a license to reuse** (for example, content from your own accounts, your clients' accounts under a written mandate, licensed content, or content whose creator granted you documented reuse rights).

When adding a source profile you must attest that you hold such rights. The Service records this attestation. ContentLoop never asserts or implies that content is owned by you when it is not, and publishing third-party content without permission may violate copyright law and platform terms.

## 2. Prohibited uses

You must not use the Service to:

- Import or publish content you do not have the right to reuse.
- Circumvent or attempt to circumvent any platform's authentication, private-account restrictions, CAPTCHA, security controls, access restrictions, or rate limits. The Service provides no such functionality, and attempting to add it or misuse the Service toward that end is prohibited.
- Access non-public or private accounts or data.
- Misrepresent the origin or authorship of content; remove attribution where it is legally or contractually required.
- Publish unlawful, infringing, deceptive, or harmful content.
- Resell scraped data or use ingested content outside the publishing workflows the rights holder authorized.
- Interfere with the Service's operation or other customers' use of it.

## 3. Third-party services

Ingestion runs exclusively through the official Apify API and the Actor you configure; publishing and analytics run exclusively through the official Metricool API using your own Metricool account. You are responsible for complying with Apify's, Metricool's, and each destination platform's (e.g. Instagram/Meta) terms of service, developer policies, and applicable law in your jurisdiction.

## 4. Your responsibility

You are solely responsible for ensuring that all imported and published content can legally be reused, for the accounts you connect, and for the publishing decisions you approve or automate. AI-generated scores, insights, and recommendations are predictions and suggestions, not guarantees, and do not constitute legal advice.

## 5. Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate this AUP, remove infringing content, and respond to valid takedown requests (including DMCA notices sent to the contact address in the Terms of Service). We may report unlawful activity to authorities where required.
